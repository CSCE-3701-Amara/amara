## 8. Risk Register and FMEA

Owner: TBD | Status: Draft | Last updated: 2026-10-14 | Jira: TBD

### 8.1 Method

This is an initial design-stage risk assessment for the AMARA hiring platform. Likelihood and impact use a 1–10 scale (10 is highest). For FMEA, severity (S), occurrence (O), and detectability (D) use the same scale, where a high D means the failure is harder to detect. RPN = S × O × D. Scores are initial estimates for review by the named epic/operations owner, not measured production rates. Until the team adopts a different threshold, an RPN of 100 or more is treated as requiring a tracked mitigation before the affected production capability is released.

### 8.4 Review and ownership

The epic owner named in each row is responsible for confirming the rating, maintaining the mitigation, and providing test or operational evidence before the affected feature is released. High-RPN items should be reviewed first. Re-score risks after major architecture changes, incident findings, or observed pilot data; do not treat initial estimates as measured probabilities.
