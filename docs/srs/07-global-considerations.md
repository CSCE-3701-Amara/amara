## 7. Global, cultural, social, environmental, economic, health, safety and welfare considerations

Owner: TBD | Status: Draft | Last updated: 2026-10-14 | Jira: TBD

This section records project impacts and design responses that affect more than a single feature. Resulting controls are traced to the current functional or non-functional requirements. These considerations do not add unstated hiring policy or replace the employer's responsibility to make hiring decisions.

| Dimension | Relevant issue and potential effect | Requirements / design response |
|---|---|---|
| Global reach and localization | The initial operating context is Egypt, while Candidates, employers, and postings may use different languages, time zones, and currencies. Incorrect time-zone or currency interpretation can cause missed deadlines or misleading compensation comparisons. | Support Arabic (`ar-EG`, RTL) and English; format dates/times/numbers for the viewer; store timestamps in UTC and use `Africa/Cairo` as the default display zone (NFR-COMP-004, NFR-COMP-013–015, NFR-ENV-020). JOB displays posting text and salary in the declared language/currency without silently translating or converting it (FR-JOB-009–012, FR-JOB-057). |
| Cultural | Hiring language, names, and local employment terminology vary. Automatically translating employer-authored criteria could alter their meaning; inferring culture or personality from assessment behavior risks unfair treatment. | Preserve posting text as authored (FR-JOB-009). Candidate insights and Amara descriptions must stay grounded in assessed evidence and avoid unsupported traits (FR-INVW-064, FR-INVW-103). Localize interface controls and direction without rewriting posting content (NFR-COMP-004, FR-JOB-022). |
| Social and fairness | Hiring outcomes affect access to employment. An automated screen, score, integrity flag, or analytics insight may disadvantage a group if the criteria or data are biased or weakly validated. | Must-have screening uses employer-configured structured criteria (FR-JOB-094–108); ANA includes demographic score-variance auditing (ANA scope); INVW excludes specified identity/demographic attributes from ranking and insight inputs (FR-INVW-065). Hiring Managers retain the decisions assigned to them; integrity flags and analytics support review rather than silently changing status (FR-INVW-053, FR-INVW-057, FR-INVW-064; UC-INVW-011). |
| Privacy and dignity | Applications contain identity, resumes, assessment answers, video, interview notes, and telemetry. Exposure before an anonymity reveal, overly broad company access, or unclear retention could harm Candidates and employers. | Use AUTH role/company-scoped access; enforce INVW anonymity at all hiring views and preserve the configured reveal behavior (FR-AUTH-004, FR-AUTH-016, FR-INVW-071–073). Apply privacy notice, data minimization, region, retention, processor, and deletion controls (NFR-PRIV-001–027). Keep Candidate-to-Candidate communication out of scope (`CON-008`). |
| Environmental | Video responses, retained resumes, telemetry, logs, and AI calls consume storage, compute, and network capacity. Unbounded retention or ingestion can increase cost and resource use. | Use lifecycle tiering for older video objects, scheduled deletion at retention, a bounded T1 log-ingest cap, and spend monitoring (NFR-CAP-005, NFR-CAP-015–020, NFR-SCAL-005, NFR-SCAL-018–019). AI inference is an external API; no T1 host GPU is required (NFR-ENV-018–019). |
| Economic | The platform is intended to be feasible for an early-stage team and small employers as it grows. Expensive video and external-provider use may exceed the operating budget; unexplained candidate-facing failures may also waste application effort. | Define measurable T1 storage, email, concurrency, and spend targets; load-test capacity; retry provider failures without discarding committed submissions (NFR-CAP-001–020, NFR-SCAL-003–006, NFR-REL-007, NFR-COMP-018–019). The $150/student-credit budget is an engineering operating constraint, not a user fee or a promise of product pricing (NFR-SCAL-005). |
| Health and welfare | Time-limited assessments and uncertain outcomes may cause stress or disadvantage people using assistive technology or unreliable networks. | Save and restore assessment responses, auto-submit saved answers on expiry, and allow Hiring Managers to grant extensions as specified (NFR-REL-008, NFR-REL-022–023, FR-INVW-011–015). Provide Arabic/English and the accessibility targets in `06-nfr.md` (NFR-COMP-004, NFR-ACC-001–004, NFR-ACC-006–018). Report results and feedback according to the configured disclosure rules (FR-INVW-075–081; FR-CAND-015–026). |
| Safety and public welfare | Fraudulent companies, impersonation, leaked Candidate identity, or incorrect status changes can expose users to harm and undermine trust in hiring decisions. | Verify company requests and interviewer-company association; support company/Candidate reporting (FR-AUTH-013–015, FR-AUTH-020–021, FR-AUTH-040). Protect identity and tenant boundaries (FR-AUTH-004, FR-INVW-071, FR-INVW-106; NFR-SEC-013–015, NFR-SEC-044–045). Make consequential advancement, rejection, and offer states traceable through the responsible workflow and notifications (FR-INVW-058, FR-INVW-107–118). |

The product's privacy, accessibility, localization, security, and reliability requirements provide measurable verification criteria for these impacts. Remaining policy choices—such as employer selection of job criteria and final human decisions—remain with the responsible employer and are not automated by the platform beyond the configured workflow rules.
